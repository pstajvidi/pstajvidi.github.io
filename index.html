Selected projects

Public health

Simulating COVID-19 in Orange County

How much does the shape of a social network change an epidemic simulation? I modeled transmission across different contact networks and compared the simulated outbreaks with historical county data.

Modeling approach

I used NetworkX to generate Barabási–Albert and Holme–Kim networks, then ran repeated agent-based simulations in Repast4py. Comparing 100-day trajectories let me examine how adding local clusters changed the shape of an outbreak.

Energy systems

Who generates their own electricity?

At the Power Transformation Lab, I use federal energy records to identify commercial and industrial plants that may supply their own electricity and examine how they vary by state and fuel.

Working with the data

I organize EIA records into one row per plant per year. Sector codes help identify candidates. To estimate how much electricity a facility supplies for itself, I also need to account for power coming in and power used on site.

Software engineering

Kamino student data center

Our team built a self-service portal for provisioning virtual machines for workshops and competitions. I contributed to the React and TypeScript interface, integration testing, and Kubernetes deployment.

Inside the portal

The frontend connects to a C++ API that manages virtual machines through Proxmox. My work included tracing requests across the interface and backend to debug integration issues before deployment.
